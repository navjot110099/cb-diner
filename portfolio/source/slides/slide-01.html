Concept project · QR ordering web app

CB Diner

Table ordering for a Park Avenue steakhouse.

Guests scan the QR code on their table and order from their phone. The kitchen sees every ticket arrive live with a bell, and the owner changes the menu mid-service. It installs like an app and keeps working offline.

Scope

Product design & full-stack build

Website, QR menu, live order status, kitchen screen, admin
