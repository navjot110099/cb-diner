Feature 02

To the kitchen, or to WhatsApp.

Guests review their order, add notes and send it, while prices are always rechecked on the server.

1

Edit right in the order

Steppers on each line, a bin icon on the last one, and a live subtotal.

2

Notes for the kitchen

Steak temperature, allergies or sauce on the side, plus the table and the guest's name.

3

Place order

Saved to Supabase with prices recalculated on the server. Dishes that just sold out are removed with a message.

4

Send on WhatsApp instead

Opens wa.me with the order prefilled, even when the signal is too weak to place it.

5

Live order status

The guest's page moves from Received to In the kitchen to Served.
