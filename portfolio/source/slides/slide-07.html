Feature 04

Change the menu mid-service.

Mark a dish sold out, fix a price or swap a photo, and guests see the change the next time they tap.

1

One-tap Sold out

The switch hides a dish from ordering instantly. Sold-out rows dim and their label flips.

2

Instant confirmation

The screen updates instantly while the save runs, and a toast confirms each change.

3

Full dish editor

Name, description, section, price, labels, vegetarian and availability, all checked on the server.

4

Photos resized on the device

Center-cropped to a ~60 KB WebP plus a square thumbnail before upload, with a JPEG fallback for Safari.
