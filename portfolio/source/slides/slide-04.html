Feature 01

Scan the table. Order in seconds.

Each table's QR code opens the menu with the table already set: no app, no sign-up, no waiting to catch a server.

1

Table number from the QR

/menu?table=5 sets the table for the visit, even from the offline cache.

Sections that follow you

Sticky chips on phones, a side rail on desktop. Scroll-spy keeps the current section highlighted.

3

Dietary marks & labels

V, VG and GF badges, plus Signature, Chef’s selection and For the table.

4

One tap to add

Add turns into a stepper. Search and a Vegetarian filter sit above the menu.

5

Order always in view

A docked panel on desktop, a floating order bar on phones.
