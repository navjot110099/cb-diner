Feature 03

Orders land with a bell, not a shout.

A live board for the line. New tickets ring, timers warn, and one tap moves an order on.

1

Live updates

Supabase Realtime on the orders table, limited to staff by row-level security. Demo mode syncs every 3 s.

2

Bell, buzz & stay awake

A bell synthesised with Web Audio, plus vibration and Screen Wake Lock for kitchen tablets.

3

Timers that warn

Tickets turn amber at 10 minutes and red at 20.

4

Guest notes stand out

Temperatures and occasions sit in a highlighted strip on the ticket.

5

One-tap status

Start / fire → Mark served → Undo. Changes show instantly and are saved in the background.
